CLIENT
Haso is a manufacturer of paper-based
environmentally friendly single use products

CUSTOMER CHALLENGE
To disrupt a wasteful industry by influencing global
distributors and getting them to switch to an
environmentally friendly alternative that Haso
offers.

DESIGN APPROACH
I created assets that present the problem of plastic
waste and emphasize the role the product plays
in the solution. I used the motif of origami and
combined imagery of nature to instantly connect
the viewer with the message.

OUTCOME
Brand assets that communicate innovation and
environmental awareness that help establish a
global presence and influence people to make
change.
